Online and offline, working together

Online brings reach. Offline builds trust.

Online

  • WhatsApp / Telegram: core hub, Gig of the Day
  • Instagram Reels: "How I earned ₹X", scam-spotting tips
  • LinkedIn: member stories, internships
  • Discord: skill rooms for design, writing, video, coding
  • YouTube Shorts: 60-second gig tutorials

Offline

  • Campus pop-up desk: QR code, spin-the-wheel, portfolio tips
  • Gig Jam events: 2 hours to build a portfolio piece or pitch a brand
  • Club partnerships: E-Cells, placement cells, design and media clubs
  • Local tie-ups: cafés, coaching centres and boutiques hire students
  • Posters: "Scan to Earn" QR in libraries and canteens
